Breathing Meditation

Slow your breath. Settle your mind. Begin again.

This practice guides simple breathing awareness to help ease tension, sharpen focus, and restore a sense of calm in the body and mind.

1. Inhale Slowly

Breathe in gently through the nose and feel the chest and belly expand.

2. Exhale Fully

Let the breath out slowly and release any buildup of stress or noise.

3. Stay Present

Rest in the rhythm of your breath and return to the moment with ease.
